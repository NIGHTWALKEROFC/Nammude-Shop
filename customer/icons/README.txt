Put your shop logo here as two PNG files:

  icon-192.png   (192x192 pixels)
  icon-512.png   (512x512 pixels)

These are used for the browser tab icon and the "Add to Home Screen" icon.
Square image, simple design, works well small (the shop logo is a good choice).
Any free tool like https://favicon.io or https://realfavicongenerator.net
can resize a logo into these sizes.

The admin site also reuses icon-192.png as its default logo placeholder.
